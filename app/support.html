Help

Need a hand with a printable, a purchase or your account? Email support@intiesltd.com. Tell us which pack you mean and what happened. Please do not send card details.

Find a pack you bought

Open the printable drawer in the app and tap Restore my purchases. Use the same Apple or Google store account you used to buy it. If a PDF still will not open, email us with the pack name and the purchase ID shown in My data.

Club billing

You can manage or cancel Club in your Apple or Google subscription settings. Deleting your Lovely Little Days email account does not cancel a store subscription.

Your data

Read our Privacy page and Data deletion page. You can delete an email account in the app’s My data screen or ask us for help by email.
