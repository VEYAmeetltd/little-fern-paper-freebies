Data deletion

Updated 8 October 2026

You can remove your Lovely Little Days email account and drawings here. We do not make child profiles.

Remove local data

Uninstall the app to clear its local catalogue and downloaded cache. If you saved a PDF elsewhere on your device, remove that copy yourself.

Delete your email account

If you signed in by email, use Delete my Lovely Little Days account below. This removes your email account, sign-in sessions and drawings linked to that account. It does not cancel a Club subscription or remove Apple or Google payment records. You may be able to restore eligible purchases using the same store account.

Ask us to remove purchase data

Email support@intiesltd.com with the purchase ID shown below and say 'Delete my Lovely Little Days data'. We will check the request, explain any record we must keep by law, and reply within one month. Removing purchase data may affect access to previously bought packs, so we will explain this before doing it.

Remove a drawing

Email support@intiesltd.com with the purchase ID shown in My data and tell us which drawing you submitted. We will hide it while we check your request, then remove the image. You can also report any published drawing in the gallery for immediate review.

Store records

Apple and Google keep their own payment records. Ask the store directly about those records or refunds. We cannot delete a store's records from this app.
