Data deletion

Updated 8 October 2026

There is no Lovely Little Days account or child profile to close.

Remove local data

Uninstall the app to clear its local catalogue and downloaded cache. If you saved a PDF elsewhere on your device, remove that copy yourself.

Ask us to remove purchase data

Email support@intiesltd.com with the purchase ID shown below and say 'Delete my Lovely Little Days data'. We will check the request, explain any record we must keep by law, and reply within one month. Removing purchase data may affect access to previously bought packs, so we will explain this before doing it.

Store records

Apple and Google keep their own payment records. Ask the store directly about those records or refunds. We cannot delete a store's records from this app.
