Privacy

Updated 8 October 2026

Lovely Little Days is published by INTIES Ltd. Questions about your data? Email support@intiesltd.com.

What we use

You can browse and get the two free pages without an account. We do not ask for a child's name, location or contacts. On Android, we ask your date of birth once before showing ads. We save only whether you are 18 or over on your device, not the date itself. If you choose email sign-in, we keep your email address, a random account ID and temporary sign-in records. Your one-time code expires after 10 minutes and your phone session after 30 days. To deliver paid packs or a club membership, we check that random ID and your store purchase status. Apple or Google handles payment details; we do not receive your card number. If you submit a child's drawing, check it contains no faces, names, school details or addresses.

Who handles it

RevenueCat checks purchases. Our INTIES email service sends sign-in codes and keeps account details. Our private download service checks access before sending a PDF. Our gallery service privately holds submitted drawings for review. It removes image metadata before storage and shows approved drawings without a name. It also records a protected version of the app purchase ID to limit submissions and hearts, and keeps reports for review. GitHub hosts catalogue images and the free-page website. Android users who confirm they are adults may see Google AdMob ads. AdMob receives an IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device or app identifiers to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. We request only G-rated, non-personalised ads after the region's consent flow. Children and users of unknown age see no ads. We do not show third-party ads on iPhone or iPad in this version. Some processing may happen outside the UK under the providers' safeguards.

Why and how long

We use purchase information to supply and restore packs and membership access, and limited technical records to keep downloads working and prevent misuse. Drawings stay private until approved. A reported drawing is hidden for another review. We keep gallery records while needed to run the gallery and respond to reports; contact us to withdraw a drawing or request deletion. Eligible adult Android users may see a labelled Google banner in the catalogue or gallery. Members see no third-party ads. We do not send marketing email or allow gallery comments in this version.

Your choices

See My data in the app for your purchase ID and unlocked packs. Use Manage ad privacy choices in the app's Privacy screen to revisit available ad choices. You can delete your email account in My data, or email us for a full copy, correction or deletion. Deleting the app removes its local cache; it does not remove store purchase records. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
